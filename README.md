# 🎣 Kiwi Catch

A free fishing game that teaches **real New Zealand fishing rules** — catch, measure, then decide: keep or release?

**▶ Play it here: https://an009123.github.io/kiwi-catch/**

No downloads, no sign-up. It runs in your phone browser as a single HTML file, and your progress saves automatically.

## How to play

1. **Start at the Bay of Islands** — the first level.
2. Cast, hook a fish, fight it in, then **measure it**.
3. Check the real rules for that area: minimum size and daily limit. Keep a legal fish, release an undersized one — just like the real thing.
4. Every spot has a ⭐ **signature fish**. Catch it to clear the level and unlock the next city.
5. Fill your collection (图鉴) with all **40 species**, earn coins and bait, and work your way through **28 spots** — from the Bay of Islands to the Chatham Islands, Niue, Tokelau and the Ross Dependency.

## Features

- Real MPI / Fisheries NZ minimum sizes and daily bag limits, different for every fishing area (SNA1 snapper rules, Challenger blue cod, Kaikōura's combined limit, no-take reserves and more)
- 40 species with illustrated artwork, from snapper and blue cod to striped marlin and Antarctic toothfish
- Live local weather and time-of-day at the spot you're fishing
- Two round modes: classic 10 casts, or a 3-minute sprint
- Daily quests, a tackle shop, and three difficulty levels

## A note on the rules

Kiwi Catch is a game, built for learning and fun. Rules are based on published MPI / Fisheries NZ recreational fishing rules, but they change — always check the free **NZ Fishing Rules app** before you head out for real.
